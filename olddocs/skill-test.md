## Skill

Enables you to assign skill requirements, such as language fluency or product expertise, to incoming calls so they can be distributed to agents with a matching set of skills.

Creating and managing Skill requires an administrator role and the appropriate `cjp:config_write` or `cjp:config_read` scopes.
